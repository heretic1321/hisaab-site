Privacy policy

Hisaab is used by one person, its owner, on their own computer.

  • What it accesses: read-only access (gmail.readonly) to the owner's Gmail, limited in code to bank, card and statement senders the owner approves; and one Google Sheet the owner shares with it.
  • What it does with it: parses transaction alerts and statements into a ledger stored on the owner's Mac and in the owner's Google Sheet.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. It never leaves the owner's devices and Google account, except that the owner may choose to have uncategorised transactions classified by an AI model under the owner's own account.
  • Never: Hisaab never sends email, moves money, or stores passwords, PINs or OTPs.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hisaab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
